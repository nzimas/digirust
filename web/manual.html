Firmware manual

Generator parameters and performance controls on the Digitone II.

Generator operation

The SYSTEM menu gains Patch Generator, Pattern Generator, Kit Generator and Phrase Generator. Choose a generator and path. YES generates while keeping that screen or menu selection available for another result.

On a percentage-control screen, press DATA/LEVEL to edit, turn it to adjust, then press again to finish. NO cancels an active edit; outside editing it returns to the parent menu. DATA never generates.

Paths and scope

Generator Scope Paths and controls
Patch Selected audio track sound; MIDI tracks are refused. Abstract, Rhythmic → IDM / Noise, Melodic, Free. Brutality on Abstract, IDM and Noise.
Kit All 16 sounds. MIDI tracks become generated audio tracks. Abstract, Rhythmic → IDM / Noise, Melodic, Free. Independent Brutality on IDM and Noise.
Pattern Complete sequence, with tempo and compressor generation. Abstract, Rhythmic → IDM / Noise, Melodic. Density on every path; Brutality on IDM and Noise.
Phrase Selected track sequence; other sequences preserved. Abstract, Rhythmic, Melodic. No parameter-editing screen or rhythmic submenu in DigiRust.

Abstract

Evolving textures, drones, effects and electroacoustic material. No abstract track has a permanent timbral archetype.

Rhythmic

IDM generates percussion and rhythmic variations. Rhythmic Noise applies stronger distortion, modulation and reduction to sounds, with noise-oriented sequencing.

Melodic and Free

Melodic generates pitched sounds and sequences. Free randomizes sound parameters within bounds; it is available in Patch and Kit only.

Density and Brutality

Density influences active tracks and note-event population. Brutality shapes source complexity, modulation, drive and reduction in sounds, or the behavior of rhythmic sequences. It is not just a volume control.

Percentages run from 0–100. Pattern Density starts at 30%; exposed Brutality controls start at 35%. Each tool/path retains its own values during operation; they reset on restart. Percentage bars invert text contrast under the fill.

Kit generation preserves the sequence and non-sound kit settings, including the compressor. Pattern generation has separate compressor logic. Abstract kits retain fixed 35% sound treatment in DigiRust; Melodic and Free kit behavior is unchanged.

Rhythmic sound roles

Tracks 1–8 retain percussion roles, including kick on 1. Tracks 9–12 independently draw abstract roles and families; 13–16 independently draw melodic roles and families. Upper tracks have no permanent bass or lead assignment. Abstract and Melodic patch paths work independently of track position.

Control All macros

Hold TRACK and turn an encoder. The macro reaches corresponding parameter positions across every subpage of SYN, FILTER, AMP and FX. Releasing TRACK leaves edits in place; stock TRACK + NO undo remains available.

Drone latch and Perform Kit

With Grid Recording disabled, hold an audio track's live trig first, then tap FUNC. Release both to retain the gate. Repeat to unlatch; STOP releases all latches. FUNC-first stock gestures remain intact. Independently decaying sound components can still fade.

Enable Perform Kit with FUNC + PRESET/KIT, modify the parameters, then exit with the same gesture. Restored parameters reach latched voices without another note-on. Oscillator phase, envelopes and effect tails are not rewound. Generators release active latches before updating content.

PAGE shortcuts

Hold PAGE and press a second key. PAGE alone, and each key without PAGE, keep their stock behavior. With FUNC held first, the generator shortcuts are off.

PAGE + Result
TRIG New patch on the selected audio track.
SYN New kit, after NEW KIT? YES/NO.
FILTER New phrase on the selected track, with its own length and clock.
AMP New pattern, after NEW PATTERN? Y/N.
Trig key 1–16 Selects that track and rolls its parameter locks, velocities and cycle conditions.
FUNC (held) Pattern break while held.
KEYBOARD (held) Shuffle while held.
TRK (held) Gate while held.
MOD On the Compressor page: copies the compressor to the bank.

Generator shortcuts

Each press draws its path from Abstract, IDM and Rhythmic Noise (the phrase shortcut has its own engine, below), never repeating the previous path, with Density and Brutality drawn per press. The values on the SYSTEM generator screens are not read or changed. Whatever the path, track 1 is always a kick, tracks 2–12 are strike sounds (snares, claps, hats, rides, cymbals, toms, rims, clangs, slams, shakers, short noises) and tracks 13–16 are textures, atmospheres, drones and pads; the path only colours them. Strikes have a fast attack, no sustain and no pitch LFO. Each sound is named after its role, so the pattern, phrase and break shortcuts treat it as what it is: PAGE + AMP gives textures long held notes, and PAGE + FILTER writes a phrase in the sound's own role. A clock from power-on stirs the draws, so a new session gives new kits. The SYSTEM menu generators are unchanged. Kit and pattern questions stay on screen until answered: YES generates, NO or any other key cancels. Generated patches and kits have no delay send; reverb and chorus sends remain.

Phrase shortcut

PAGE + FILTER writes a phrase on the selected track in the role of its sound, and sets that track's own length (2–32 steps) and clock (2x, 3/2x, 1x, 3/4x, 1/2x, 1/4x, 1/8x). Some phrases stay locked to the bar with a new feel (24 steps at 3/2x for triplets, 32 at 2x, 12 at 3/4x); others drift as polymeter at lengths such as 5, 7, 13, 21 or 31. Kick and snare always lock to the pattern's meter; textures, drones and pads are held on slow clocks.

Strike sounds draw one of twelve rhythm families: Euclidean, aksak, ghost-weighted, Thue-Morse and Fibonacci, a cellular automaton played as cycle conditions, tresillo/clave/bembé, power-noise hammering, industrial stomp, broken IDM with flams, four-on-the-floor and backbeats. Then ghosts, rolls, swing/drag/push, cycle variation, a turnaround, a few locks and tom pitches. The kick keeps step 1 and there is no other pitch movement. Everything is ordinary step data and can be edited afterwards. On a one-length pattern the phrase switches to per-track mode; every other track keeps its length and clock. The SYSTEM menu Phrase Generator is unchanged.

Lock roll

Locks are drawn from the sound processing, never the source, so the sound remains recognizable: filter, amp envelope and pan, overdrive and bit/sample-rate reduction, LFOs with non-pitch destinations, and sends, with occasional delay throws. Each trig gets a velocity, and some trigs a cycle condition (1:2 … 4:4; the first trig stays unconditional). Pitch is never changed; note lengths only occasionally, within the gap to the next trig. Each press replaces the previous roll on that track. MIDI tracks are refused.

Break

Hold PAGE, then hold FUNC. The pattern plays a break for as long as FUNC is held: stutter, half time, double time, reverse, chop, roll or slam, with ratchets and flams on drum tracks. Release either key and the pattern returns exactly as it was. Each press draws a different character. Drones and the kit are left alone. FUNC first, then PAGE, keeps its stock meaning.

Break, shuffle and gate follow the length that actually plays: the pattern LENGTH in one-length mode, each track's own length in per-track mode. A 12-step loop is three beats of four; shuffle pieces and gate slices never cross a beat, a track of another length follows its own loop, and steps past the played length are never touched. Patterns in 4/4 break exactly as before.

Shuffle

Hold PAGE, then hold KEYBOARD. While both are held, the tracks trade their sequencing: each track with notes plays another track's trigs (steps, velocity, length, micro timing, conditions, retrigs and locks) on its own sound and at its own pitches. The trade is redrawn in half beats, beats or half bars, never across a beat; tracks of the same length and clock trade together. Release either key and every track plays its own steps again; nothing is saved. MIDI and empty tracks are left alone. Breaks and shuffles do not stack.

Gate

Hold PAGE, then hold TRK. While both are held, the tracks with notes are muted and opened rhythmically, in slices shared by all tracks so the gating stays in time: all sixteenths, sixteenths and eighths, or a broken mix up to quarter notes, drawn per press with a new gating each time. Every slice keeps at least one track playing and silences at least one. A silenced step plays nothing; notes already sounding ring on, so releases and delay and reverb tails are kept. Release either key and the pattern returns exactly. MIDI and empty tracks are left alone; trig keys do nothing while it is on. Break, shuffle and gate do not stack.

Compressor to bank

Open the Compressor page (FUNC + MOD, “Compressor (1/5)”), hold PAGE and press MOD. All nine compressor controls are copied to every other pattern in the current bank of 16 that has notes: COMP APPLIED: n PATTERNS. Empty patterns and other banks are untouched, and each pattern’s compressor can still be adjusted afterwards. The copies are unsaved edits until the project is saved. On other pages PAGE + MOD behaves like MOD. It does nothing while a Perform kit is active.

Note audition

In grid recording, hold a trig and turn NOTE on the TRIG page. Each new pitch sounds at once, on the track’s sound at the step’s velocity, one note per detent (the whole chord if the step holds one). Releasing the trig stops it. Nothing is written by the audition itself.
