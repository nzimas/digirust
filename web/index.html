Musical direction

For the edges of electronic music.

IDM, rhythmic noise, industrial, electroacoustic and acousmatic music guide the tools. Percussion sits alongside evolving textures, noise, sparse gestures and unusual pitched sounds.

That is the author's focus. Conventional groovebox workflows and mainstream beatmaking are outside the project's design goals.

On the instrument

Four ways to start something new.

01 / PATCH

One sound, another possibility.

Generate a patch for the selected audio track. Abstract, rhythmic, melodic and Free paths explore different kinds of sound.

02 / KIT

A palette across sixteen tracks.

Generate all 16 sounds together. IDM and Rhythmic Noise have independent Brutality controls for shaping the result.

03 / PHRASE

Compose one layer at a time.

Replace the selected track's sequence and keep the others. Build and refine a pattern through individual phrases.

04 / PATTERN

Structure with room to move.

Generate a complete pattern. Density controls its population; rhythmic Brutality influences intensity and experimentation.
